## Root cause

The recorder took its record before the view transition was ready.

- **What the harness did.** After a step, `takeMotion` in `scripts/boards/capture.ts` waited for three animation frames and 60 ms. Then `motionTake` in `scripts/boards/inpage.js` swept the page's animations and took (and cleared) the record. A view transition's pseudo-element animations, `theme-reveal` on `::view-transition-new(root)` and the UA's `-ua-view-transition-group-anim-root`, do not exist until the transition is `ready`. That comes after its old state is captured and its DOM update has run.
- **Why a fixed wait does not cover it.** Chromium keeps running frames while a transition is pending. In the investigation probe, with the reveal's update held back for 400 ms, the three frame waits and the 60 ms timer ran out, and the record was taken 101 ms after the click. `ready` came 444 ms after the click. When `ready` lands after the take, the record holds neither motion. Both animations then start into a record nobody takes. `hold()` waits for `ready` on its own, so the reveal is still held and measured, and no other line fails. This matches the failing run exactly (`uiv-PRV-2-ac48f3ab`, e390ca3): those two "does not run" lines and nothing else, with the host under load.
- **Deterministic reproduction.** "records theme-reveal motion under the reproduced timing condition" (`e2e/fidelity-harness.spec.ts`) injects `LATE_REVEAL` into the fixture site. It holds the dark row's reveal back until the harness next calls its recorder, so `ready` always comes after the fixed wait, at any host speed. The harness as PRV-22 merged it (f0f08ab, run from `git archive`) then reports exactly the failing run's lines:
  - `fx/header [theme-reveal, 1440, dark] motion page theme-reveal: expected grow@button[Switch to the dark theme] 560ms, got does not run`
  - `fx/header [theme-reveal, 1440, dark] motion page -ua-view-transition-group-anim-root: expected none 250ms, got does not run`
- **A second dependence on host speed, same path.** The `animationstart` listener skips `-ua-` names, so only the take-time sweep notes the 250 ms group animation, and the sweep skips finished animations. Under CDP `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate` 40, the pre-fix harness lost the group animation alone (`theme-reveal` was still recorded).
- **Ruled out.** The click does not land before the handler: the handler is attached at `DOMContentLoaded`, `load()` waits for the load event, and the probe logs `startViewTransition` in the same millisecond as the click. Chromium skipping the transition has no host-speed trigger on this page. Its only timeout is on the DOM update (measured: rejected after 4002 ms, "Transition was aborted because of timeout in DOM update"), and the fixture's update runs synchronously.

**Fix.**

- **Wait for the transition.** `takeMotion` now awaits the new in-page `transitionReady()` before taking the record. It waits however long a transition the page has started takes to be ready (or skipped) and confirmed.
- **Note at ready, provisionally.** The recorder notes a transition's pseudo-element animations as soon as `ready` resolves, so the short group animation cannot finish before a sweep sees it. A note counts only once its animation has played: it finished before teardown (its `finished` promise resolved), or it was still live two frames after `ready`, or it is live when the record is taken. A reveal the page skips once it is ready never plays: Chromium cancels its animations to `idle` at once, which rejects `finished`, so it still counts as "does not run". A short transition that is not held does count, even when it has played and been torn down (cancelled to `idle`) before the recorder's frames come round (Sol recheck at bc0561c).
- **Hold at ready.** For an in-flight state, `captureState` arms the midpoint hold (`holdArm(IN_FLIGHT_AT)`) before the step runs. The page holds the reveal's animations the moment the transition is ready, so neither can finish before the driver reaches `hold()`. The held frame is the same one `hold()` produced before: the same `currentTime` for each animation.
- **Results.** Under the reproduction, the fixed harness records both motions at 560 ms and 250 ms and holds both animations paused at `IN_FLIGHT_AT` (0.5). They are still held there when the driver resumes 800 ms after the reveal is ready, longer than either animation runs. With CPU throttling at 40 and at 100, it recorded both motions and held the frame at 0.5.
- **Not loosened.** With no view transition at all, or with the reveal skipped in the page's ready callback, the theme still switches and both motions are still reported as "does not run".
